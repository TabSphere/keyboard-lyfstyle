Turn the keyboard on

iPhone: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Lyfstyle. While typing, hold the 🌐 globe and choose Lyfstyle.

Android: open Lyfstyle → Keyboard tab → tap Open keyboard settings and switch Lyfstyle on, then Show keyboard picker and choose it.

My photo or colours don't show on the iPhone keyboard

iPhone only lets a keyboard see things saved by its app when Allow Full Access is on: Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Lyfstyle → Allow Full Access. Then tap 🎨 on the keyboard and choose Your photo or My colours. The bottom of the 🎨 panel shows whether Full Access is on, whether your photo was found and whether Pro is active.

Some letters look like boxes

Styled letters are Unicode characters. A few apps or older phones don't have every symbol, so a letter can show as a box. Try another style, or check how it looks in the app you're posting to.

Restore Lifetime Pro on a new phone

Sign in with the same Apple ID or Google account you bought with, open Lyfstyle → Settings → Restore purchases. Pro is a one-off purchase: there is no subscription to cancel.

Refunds

Payments are handled by Apple and Google, so refunds are requested through them: reportaproblem.apple.com for iPhone, or Google Play → Payments & subscriptions → Budget & history for Android.

Translate and voice typing

Tap 文A on the keyboard to translate your message. On iPhone this needs iOS 26 or later and the language downloaded in your iPhone's settings. On Android, each language downloads once (about 30 MB). Voice typing is on Android's 🎙 key; on iPhone use the 🎙 at the bottom right of the screen.
