Privacy policy

Lyfstyle: Fonts Keyboard · Last updated 22 November 2026

The short version: Lyfstyle has no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. We do not collect, store or sell what you type, your photos or any personal data. The app and keyboard work on your phone; the only outside services involved are your phone's own (Apple's or Google's), explained below.

Who we are

Lyfstyle is made by TabSphere Ltd, London, United Kingdom ("we"). For anything about this policy, contact us at info@tabsphere.co.uk.

What you type

The Lyfstyle keyboard turns the letters you type into styled Unicode characters on your device and passes them to the app you are typing in. We never receive, store or send your keystrokes. The keyboard has no text prediction or "learning", and keeps no record of what you type.

In password, email, web address, phone, number and one-time-code fields the keyboard always types plain text.

In the Lyfstyle app, the text you are styling is saved only in the app's storage on your phone so it is still there next time. You can clear it in Settings.

Your photo and colours

If you choose a photo as your keyboard background, the app makes a smaller copy and keeps it on your phone so the keyboard can show it. The same goes for colours you mix on the colour wheel. Neither is uploaded anywhere.

iPhone "Full Access"

Typing works without Full Access. If you turn it on, the keyboard uses it only to read things the Lyfstyle app saved on your phone: your photo, your colours, your theme choice and whether you have Lifetime Pro. The keyboard does not use Full Access to send what you type anywhere.

Translate in the keyboard

Translation happens on your phone. On iPhone it uses Apple's on-device translation; you may need to download a language in your iPhone's settings first. On Android it uses Google's on-device translation (ML Kit): the first time you choose a language, that language's translation model is downloaded from Google, and after that your text is translated on the phone. The text you translate is not sent to us.

Voice typing (Android)

When you tap the microphone key, Lyfstyle uses your phone's built-in speech recognition service (usually Google's) to turn speech into text. The microphone is used only while you are speaking after tapping the key. Your speech is handled by that service under its own privacy terms; Lyfstyle does not receive or keep recordings. On iPhone, add-on keyboards cannot use the microphone, so voice typing uses Apple's own dictation instead.

Purchases

Lifetime Pro is a one-off purchase handled by the App Store or Google Play. They process the payment; we never see your card details. The app only checks with the store whether you own Pro so it can unlock it, and keeps that yes/no on your phone.

Copying and sharing

When you tap Copy or Share, the text you chose goes to your phone's clipboard or share sheet. What happens next is up to you and the app you share to.

Children

Lyfstyle is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Your choices and rights

  • Clear your saved text in the app's Settings.
  • Remove your keyboard photo on the Keyboard tab.
  • Switch to another keyboard at any time, or remove Lyfstyle in your phone's keyboard settings.
  • Uninstalling the app deletes everything it saved on your phone.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. If you have any question or request under UK or EU data protection law, email us and we will reply.

Changes

If this policy changes we will update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what data is handled, we will also say so in the app's update notes.
