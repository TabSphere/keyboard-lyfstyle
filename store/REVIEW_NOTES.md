# Store review notes

## What Lyfstyle does

Lyfstyle converts typed characters into Unicode-styled text. It does not install font files, modify other apps or provide system-wide fonts. Rendering depends on the destination app/platform; unsupported characters pass through unchanged.

## Review access

No account or sign-in is required. The composer and 12 free styles are available immediately. Pro is a non-consumable lifetime purchase with product ID `com.tabsphere.keyboardlyfstyle.pro`; pricing comes from the store product.

No purchase is required to review keyboard setup, free styles, themes, copy/share, compatibility information or privacy behaviour.

## Keyboard setup

### iOS

Settings → General → Keyboard → Keyboards → Add New Keyboard → Lyfstyle Keyboard. Use the globe/next-keyboard control to switch. Core free typing works without Full Access. Full Access is optional and relates only to the fallback App Group Pro synchronisation path being verified; the app’s final privacy copy must match the tested behaviour.

### Android

Open Lyfstyle → Keyboard → Open keyboard settings, enable Lyfstyle Keyboard, then use Choose keyboard / the system IME picker. The app also reports enabled/selected status.

## Safe fields and privacy

Password/secure, email, URL, phone, numeric and OTP inputs use Normal mode. Neither keyboard contains purchase, upgrade or rating prompts. There are no analytics, ads, accounts, predictions, autocorrect or AI rewriting. Typed content and drafts are not transmitted by Lyfstyle. Apple/Google may process purchase entitlement requests according to their own store services.

## Purchase review

Test purchase and restore using the console-provided reviewer/sandbox account. Do not hardcode or assume a price. The transaction is finished only after the expected non-consumable product reaches purchased state and ownership is persisted locally.

Record the tested build number, store environment and entitlement result in the release checklist. Do not submit these notes until purchase, restore and keyboard entitlement synchronisation have passed on physical devices.

## Content and screenshots

Use only genuine release-candidate device captures. No private messages, personal data or fake third-party app UI. The app icon and promotional assets are original Lyfstyle artwork.
