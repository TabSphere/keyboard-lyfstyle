What's new in 1.0.2 (plain text: App Store "What's New" and Google Play "Release notes", under 500 characters)

Word suggestions: finish words and guess the next one, in your font. Learns your words on your phone only.
Emoji: a full emoji panel with your recents.
Glow lights (Pro): a soft light that travels around your keyboard.
Make your own style (Pro): mix a font, an effect and a frame, and use it on the keyboard.
Keys feel better: no dead spots at the edges, a crisp tap click and buzz, and quicker taps on iPhone.
Fixes: photo backgrounds on iPhone, bottom tabs on phones with navigation buttons, and delete with text selected.
