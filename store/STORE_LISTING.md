## Full description

Say it with character.

Lyfstyle turns your words into eye-catching Unicode text for captions, bios, messages and usernames. Type once, preview every look with your own words, then copy, share or keep writing with the Lyfstyle keyboard.

WRITE YOUR WAY
• 12 free styles, from Serif Bold and Script to Monospace and Circled
• Normal Text whenever you want plain typing
• Explicit decorations in the composer, only when you choose them
• Favourites and recent styles for quick access

A KEYBOARD THAT FEELS LIKE YOU
• Native iPhone and Android keyboard experiences
• Porcelain, Ink, Mono and Blush themes
• Switch styles without leaving your conversation
• Automatic Normal mode for passwords, email, URL, phone, numeric and OTP fields

GO PRO ONCE
Unlock 12 additional styles and three Pro decorations with one lifetime purchase. Pricing is shown in your local store. No subscription.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No accounts. No ads. No analytics. Nothing you type is uploaded. Drafts and preferences stay on your device. Copy and share only when you choose.

A NOTE ABOUT COMPATIBILITY
Lyfstyle creates Unicode characters; it does not install system-wide fonts or restyle other apps. Some characters may look different or be unsupported in certain apps, platforms or fonts. Unsupported characters remain unchanged, and the composer explains compatibility as you type.
